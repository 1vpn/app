1VPN is a free VPN that changes your IP address by securely routing your internet traffic through private servers. 1VPN uses Android's VpnService to send your traffic through an encrypted tunnel.

Features
• Free VPN: Enjoy secure, private internet browsing with our free VPN tier at no cost.
• No Registration Required: Use the free VPN tier instantly without creating an account.
• Strong Encryption: Uses V2Ray/Xray with VLESS and Reality protocol, secured by X25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption.
• No-Logs Policy: Your browsing history, IP address, and personal data are never stored.
• Unlimited Bandwidth: No data caps on streaming, downloading, or browsing.
• Fast Speeds: Consistent and reliable VPN performance across all locations.
• Global Server Network: Access VPN servers in multiple countries for better speed and content access.
• Lightweight App: Optimized to run fast and efficiently on your device.
• Easy to Use: Simple, clean design that's easy for anyone to use.
• Quick Support: Friendly and responsive support team available anytime.
• One-Tap Connect: Connect with a single tap.
• Automatic: Picks the fastest available location.
• Direct Regions: Sites from a region you choose bypass the VPN. Some sites only work when you connect from the same country as the site.
• DNS Through the Tunnel: While connected, DNS goes through the tunnel. Names a Direct Region sends outside the VPN resolve on your network.
• Always-On VPN: Guidance to turn it on, so the tunnel stays up when you close the app.
• Quick Settings Tile: Disconnect from Quick Settings, or tap to open the app and connect.
• Support: The FAQ and the contact page are on the app's Support page.
• 26 Languages: With localized country and city names.

Use Cases
• Protect Your IP Address: Prevent tracking by hiding your IP from websites, advertisers, and snoopers.
• Private Browsing: Keep your online activity hidden from ISPs and network administrators. Traffic to a Direct Region you choose leaves from your real IP address.
• Bypass Internet Censorship: Access blocked websites and apps on restricted networks or in censored regions.
• Unblock Region-Locked Content: Unlock websites and services not available in your country.
